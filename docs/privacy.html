Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Bhejo collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no cloud. Files move directly between your own devices.

What Bhejo does not do

  • No account, sign-up, or login.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage statistics.
  • No cookies, local storage tracking, or fingerprinting in the browser interface.
  • No ads, no third-party SDKs, no data brokers.
  • No upload to any server we operate. There is no such server.

What stays on your device

The receiving side stores what it needs to work locally: a device name, a self-signed certificate and its fingerprint, a small trust store of devices you have accepted, and the files you choose to receive in the folder you selected. Deleting that folder and the .bhejo configuration directory removes it all.

What is visible on your network

While Bhejo is running, devices on the same local network can see a discovery announcement containing the device name, address, port, and certificate fingerprint. That is how the other side finds you. Transfers are encrypted with TLS and verified against the certificate fingerprint. Nothing is broadcast beyond your local network, and nothing is sent to us.

This website

This is a static site served by GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts of our own. GitHub may record standard web server information such as IP addresses as part of serving the page; that is covered by GitHub’s own privacy statement, not by us.

Email

If you write to jdmibusiness01@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. It is used to reply to you and is not sold or added to a mailing list.

Children

Bhejo is a general-purpose file transfer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
